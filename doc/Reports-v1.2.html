The 15

1 · Platform Overview Dashboard

ADMIN Real-time · 60s cache

Question. What is the platform's current state?

Source tables. users, orders, order_items, payments, designs, product_templates, shipments, cart_items

Metrics. Customer count · designer count · printer count · published designs · active templates · orders today · orders this month · revenue today · revenue this month · pending payments · pending items · in-flight shipments · abandoned carts (last 24h).

Implementation. One Eloquent query per metric, fired in parallel. Cache the response for 60s. This is the front-page of the admin console.

Why first. It is the only place every business-critical KPI surfaces in one view. Without it, nothing else has context.

2 · Revenue by Day

ADMIN Daily

Question. What is the daily revenue trend?

Source tables. orders, order_items, payments

SELECT DATE(orders.created_at) AS day,
       COUNT(DISTINCT orders.id) AS order_count,
       SUM(order_items.unit_price * order_items.quantity) AS gross_revenue,
       SUM(order_items.quantity) AS units_sold
FROM orders
JOIN order_items ON order_items.order_id = orders.id
JOIN payments ON payments.order_id = orders.id
WHERE payments.status = 'confirmed'
  AND orders.created_at BETWEEN :from AND :to
  AND orders.deleted_at IS NULL
GROUP BY day
ORDER BY day;

Filters. Date range · payment method · shipping country · category.

Why second. The canonical "is the platform growing?" chart. Every board deck, every investor conversation.

3 · Printer Work Queue

PRINTER Real-time

Question. Which order items are waiting for me to move to the next status?

Source tables. order_items, orders, design_product_mappings, designs, product_templates, product_variants

SELECT order_items.id, order_items.status, order_items.quantity, order_items.unit_price,
       orders.id AS order_id, orders.shipping_city, orders.shipping_country,
       designs.title AS design_title, product_templates.name AS template_name,
       product_variants.attributes AS variant_attributes
FROM order_items
JOIN orders ON orders.id = order_items.order_id
JOIN design_product_mappings dpm ON dpm.id = order_items.design_product_mapping_id
JOIN designs ON designs.id = dpm.design_id
JOIN product_templates ON product_templates.id = dpm.product_template_id
LEFT JOIN product_variants ON product_variants.id = order_items.product_variant_id
WHERE order_items.printer_provider_id = :printer_id
  AND order_items.status IN ('pending','received','printing','printed')
  AND order_items.deleted_at IS NULL
ORDER BY orders.created_at ASC;

Why third. It is the only report a printer opens every day. Drives fulfillment throughput — the platform's biggest operational lever.

5 · Revenue by Designer (Top 25)

ADMIN Daily

Question. Which designers earn the platform the most revenue?

Source tables. users, designer_profiles, designs, design_product_mappings, order_items, orders, payments

SELECT users.id, users.name,
       COUNT(DISTINCT orders.id) AS order_count,
       SUM(order_items.unit_price * order_items.quantity) AS gross_revenue,
       SUM(order_items.quantity) AS units_sold
FROM orders
JOIN order_items ON order_items.order_id = orders.id
JOIN design_product_mappings dpm ON dpm.id = order_items.design_product_mapping_id
JOIN designs ON designs.id = dpm.design_id
JOIN designer_profiles ON designer_profiles.id = designs.designer_id
JOIN users ON users.id = designer_profiles.user_id
JOIN payments ON payments.order_id = orders.id
WHERE payments.status = 'confirmed'
  AND orders.created_at BETWEEN :from AND :to
GROUP BY users.id
ORDER BY gross_revenue DESC LIMIT 25;

Why fifth. Identifies the top tier of creators. Drives partnership, retention, and incentive-program decisions.

7 · Stuck Payments

OPS Hourly

Question. Which payments have been pending for more than 3 days?

Source tables. payments, orders

SELECT payments.id, payments.method, payments.created_at,
       orders.id AS order_id, orders.customer_id,
       JULIANDAY('now') - JULIANDAY(payments.created_at) AS age_days
FROM payments
JOIN orders ON orders.id = payments.order_id
WHERE payments.status = 'pending'
  AND payments.created_at < DATE('now','-3 days')
ORDER BY age_days DESC;

Why seventh. Drives admin action queue. A pending payment is either awaiting proof-of-payment (customer) or waiting on confirmation (admin). The report surfaces both buckets.

8 · Customer Lifetime Value (Top 100)

ADMIN Daily

Question. Which customers spend the most over time?

Source tables. users, orders, payments

SELECT users.id, users.name, users.email,
       COUNT(DISTINCT orders.id) AS order_count,
       SUM(orders.total_amount) AS ltv,
       MAX(orders.created_at) AS last_order_at
FROM users
JOIN orders ON orders.customer_id = users.id
JOIN payments ON payments.order_id = orders.id
WHERE payments.status = 'confirmed'
  AND users.role = 'customer' AND users.deleted_at IS NULL
GROUP BY users.id
ORDER BY ltv DESC LIMIT 100;

Why eighth. Identifies VIP customers for retention, support prioritization, and future incentive programs.

9 · Designer Payout Statement

DESIGNER Daily

Question. How much should the platform pay me?

Source tables. orders, order_items, design_product_mappings, designs, payments, settings

SELECT DATE(orders.created_at) AS day,
       SUM(order_items.unit_price * order_items.quantity) AS gross_revenue,
       SUM(order_items.unit_price * order_items.quantity) * (1 - :commission_rate) AS designer_payout,
       SUM(order_items.unit_price * order_items.quantity) * :commission_rate AS platform_fee
FROM orders
JOIN order_items ON order_items.order_id = orders.id
JOIN design_product_mappings dpm ON dpm.id = order_items.design_product_mapping_id
JOIN designs ON designs.id = dpm.design_id
JOIN payments ON payments.order_id = orders.id
WHERE payments.status = 'confirmed'
  AND designs.designer_id = :designer_id
  AND orders.created_at BETWEEN :from AND :to
GROUP BY day;

Why ninth. The single most important financial document for designers. Drives trust and retention. :commission_rate is read from settings.platform.commission_rate.

11 · Cart Abandonment

OPS Daily · 6h cache

Question. Which customers added to cart but never checked out?

Source tables. users, cart_items, orders

SELECT users.id, users.name, users.email,
       COUNT(DISTINCT cart_items.id) AS cart_items_count,
       MAX(cart_items.updated_at) AS last_cart_activity
FROM users
JOIN cart_items ON cart_items.user_id = users.id
LEFT JOIN orders ON orders.customer_id = users.id
  AND orders.created_at > cart_items.updated_at
WHERE users.role = 'customer' AND users.deleted_at IS NULL
  AND orders.id IS NULL
GROUP BY users.id
HAVING last_cart_activity < DATE('now','-7 days')
ORDER BY last_cart_activity DESC;

Why eleventh. The classic e-commerce growth lever. Identifies recoverable revenue with a simple re-engagement email.

12 · Failed / Stuck Shipments

OPS Hourly · 5min cache

Question. Which shipments are pending or in-transit for too long?

Source tables. shipments, orders

SELECT shipments.id, shipments.status, shipments.shipped_at,
       orders.id AS order_id,
       JULIANDAY('now') - JULIANDAY(shipments.created_at) AS age_days
FROM shipments
JOIN orders ON orders.id = shipments.order_id
WHERE shipments.status IN ('pending','shipped')
  AND (shipments.shipped_at IS NULL OR shipments.shipped_at < DATE('now','-7 days'))
ORDER BY age_days DESC;

Why twelfth. Customer-experience warning system. Drives proactive support before complaints arrive.

15 · Top Designs by Revenue

SELECT designs.id, designs.title, designer_profiles.id AS designer_id,

Implementation sequencing

The reports are already ordered by suggested build sequence. A practical rollout:

PhaseReportsWhy this phase
Phase 1 — MVP dashboards1, 3, 4, 13, 14The five pages every user opens every day. Without these, the platform feels broken.
Phase 2 — Revenue & payouts2, 9, 10Trust-building. Without payouts, designers and printers will not stay.
Phase 3 — Leaderboards5, 6, 15Drives healthy competition among designers and printers. Useful for admin decisions.
Phase 4 — Operations & growth7, 8, 11, 12Active management. Surfaces problems before they grow, and identifies recoverable revenue.

Conventions (apply to all 15)

  • Soft-delete: every count excludes deleted_at IS NOT NULL rows.
  • Revenue: every monetary report joins payments with payments.status = 'confirmed'. Snapshotted order_items.unit_price × quantity is the source of truth — historical price integrity is preserved.
  • Date dimension: financial metrics use payments.confirmed_at; order-count metrics use orders.created_at.
  • PII protection: designer / printer reports never expose customer email or phone; customer reports only ever show the authenticated customer's own data.
  • UUID lookups: queries filter on UUID id values where applicable (e.g. designs.designer_id). The 9 public-facing tables use CHAR(36) UUIDs per FR-15.
  • Export: every report supports ?format=json (default) and ?format=csv stream.
